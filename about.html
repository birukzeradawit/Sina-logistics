Who We Are

SINA Supplies and Logistics PLC is a diversified Ethiopian private company engaged in import and export trade, logistics, consulting, construction, real estate, agriculture, energy, tourism, manufacturing, and mining.

Our integrated business model delivers complete solutions to government institutions, private organizations, and individual customers throughout Ethiopia and international markets.

With a commitment to excellence and innovation, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner in Ethiopia's economic development, providing comprehensive services that span across nine integrated sectors.
